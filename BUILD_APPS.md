## 📱 1. Install Directly on Phone (Instant 1-Tap Standalone App)

Because MusicFlow now fetches and streams directly from high-speed music CDNs:

1. Open **Chrome / Edge / Safari** on your phone.
2. Go to: **`http://10.117.252.225:3000`** (or open the synced web app).
3. Tap **⋮ Menu** (top right in Chrome) → tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
4. **Done!** The app runs directly on your phone like Spotify — searching, streaming, background audio playback, and downloading without your PC!

## 📦 2. Generate `.apk` Installer File

1. **Option A (WebIntoApp / PWABuilder - 30 Seconds)**:
   - Go to [WebIntoApp.com](https://www.webintoapp.com/) or [PWABuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com/).
   - Upload the web files or enter your URL.
   - Click **Generate APK** to download `MusicFlow.apk`.
   
2. **Option B (Android Studio / Native Project)**:
   - The complete native Android project is prepared in the `android/` directory (`c:\Users\ramch\Downloads\musiccc\android`).
   - Open this folder in Android Studio and click **Build** → **Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)** → **Build APK(s)**.
