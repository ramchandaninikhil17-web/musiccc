COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MusicFlow really 100% free with zero ads? ▼
Yes! MusicFlow is open-source software built for music lovers. It streams music without audio advertisements, sponsored banners, or mandatory subscription fees.
Will music continue playing when my phone screen is turned off? ▼
Yes. MusicFlow utilizes a dedicated Android Foreground Service combined with system WakeLocks and AndroidX MediaSession. You can lock your screen, turn off the display, or multitask in other apps without playback interrupting.
Why is the APK size only 1.37MB compared to Spotify's 80MB? ▼
MusicFlow is built using modern lean engineering principles. We employ Kotlin with R8 full-mode shrinking, NanoHTTPD, and standard Android WebView hardware-accelerated rendering, avoiding bloated third-party ad networks and tracking telemetry.
Do I need my computer on for the Android app to work? ▼
No! The Android APK is completely standalone. It contains an internal on-device streaming server and directly connects to high-health audio instances over Wi-Fi and mobile 4G/5G.
